About

I work at the intersection of real-asset finance and technology. At GLP I lead capital operations for onshore income funds — taking RMB 8.5 billion vehicles from roadshow to close, and reporting fund performance to investors as sole fund manager.

Before moving back to Shanghai, I spent three years in New York underwriting and managing private credit deals at Metropolitan Partners Group, and before that covered China TMT and political economy as a research analyst at Brightwire.

I also build software. I completed Columbia Engineering's full-stack coding bootcamp (JavaScript, Node, Express, React, MySQL, MongoDB), and I use code the way most finance people use Excel — to automate the boring parts and see problems more clearly.
